## Beat sheet (target 10–11 min)

1. **0:00–0:30 — Cold open.** One of the two scripts above, cut against a floor-plan/render
   split screen. No channel intro, no logo sting, no "hey guys."
2. **0:30–1:00 — One-line credibility.** Who this is, in one sentence, over B-roll of the actual
   floor plan document.
3. **1:00–2:00 — Ground the stakes.** Real project, 26-room floor, this room (the control
   office) is room one, designer cleared showing it.
4. **2:00–3:00 — Name the failure mode.** The industry's real skepticism (invented windows,
   flipped furniture, wrong materials) as the antagonist. If illustrating with a bad example,
   label it illustrative — never present a staged mistake as real client work.
5. **3:00–5:30 — Pipeline walkthrough.** Fast-cut through intake → concept → extraction →
   verify → FF&E → whitebox → line-art → photoreal → refine → package. Plain-language captions,
   translate every jargon term the moment it appears.
6. **5:30–6:15 — The verify step, called out.** Its own beat — this is the actual
   differentiator. Plan-lines overlaid on the render, or a toggle between the two.
7. **6:15–7:30 — The proof moment, full reveal.** Full-screen side-by-side, slow pan, narrating
   specific match points calmly. No music swell.
8. **7:30–8:45 — The human proof.** The 8 refine passes as real client-directed iteration; state
   explicitly the designer gave permission and called it good enough for their own clients and
   vendors.
9. **8:45–9:45 — Zoom out.** Room one of 26; next 2–3 rooms get built and timed on camera with
   the designer present.
10. **9:45–10:15 — Close + single CTA** (below).

Single ask only. No link-in-bio, no booking link, no cross-platform follow ask in this video —

## Production pitfalls to avoid (from research)

- No dead air from real-time render waits — jump-cut with a caption ("...9 minutes later").
- No talking-head preamble before the proof — open cold.
- Narrate intent/outcome per pipeline step, not clicks.
- Cut every 4–8 seconds; no long static screen-recording stretches.
- Translate every jargon term (vision-LLM, FF&E, whitebox) the moment it's said.
- Deliver the proof by the midpoint — don't delay it to the end.
- One CTA, under 30 seconds.
- Restrained scoring under calm narration — no dramatic swell.
- Never stage a fake "bad render" and present it as real — directly contradicts the brand claim.
