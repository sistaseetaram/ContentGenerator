## The two audiences, and the rule that keeps them from fighting

| Audience | Who | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary — Bridge 01 ICP** | Owners of 2–15 person architecture / interior / construction studios | Outcome first. Jargon translated. "Would a busy firm owner feel respected, or sold to?" |
| **Secondary — AI peers + hiring managers** | MSc AI / AI-engineering roles, collaborators | Evidence of real engineering: verification, experiment design, honest negative results |

**The rule:** every post leads with the work or the client outcome. The engineering appears as the
*reason the outcome can be trusted* — never as the headline. A post that would only impress another
builder is a positioning leak (this is exactly why `idea-013`, the "my content engine runs on a cent
a day" flex, is parked).

Why this works rather than splitting the feed: the method IS the differentiator. "Five blocking
checks run for $0 before any paid render fires" reads as rigour to a hiring manager and as
trustworthiness to a studio owner. One sentence, both audiences. LinkedIn carries outcome-first with
the method underneath; YouTube long-form carries the deep build and doubles as portfolio evidence.

## Standing rules (every slot)

comment same day** (~30% lift — best-evidenced single lever available) · document carousel is the

## Honest risks

1. **No baseline exists.** `metrics.json` is empty and `analyzer-latest.json` is `n_posts=0`. Nobody
   can promise this lifts engagement. Realistic early expectation at this follower tier is ~5
   engagements/post at ~2.17%. Judge on saves, comment quality and ICP reach — not raw likes.
2. **Week 2 is contingent.** If the shoot slips, the number slips. The block is swappable for exactly
   this reason.
3. **Cadence risk is real.** 5 posts/week with `content-creator` still unbuilt is close to the
   configuration that stalled at 11 posts. The mitigation is batching from one recording session plus
   the 26-room series as structural supply.
4. **The backlog is stale, not empty.** `ideas.json` was last refreshed 2026-06-22 and has never
   ingested the design-automation wiki, which now holds months of proof-grade material. Refreshing it
   from that wiki is the highest-leverage ideator run available.
